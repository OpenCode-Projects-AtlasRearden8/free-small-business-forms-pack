Contract-Sanity Checklist

Before you sign anything, check the ten things that quietly hurt the most.

The ten checks

  • I can say in one sentence who owes whom what Parties, deliverable, price. If you can’t, it’s not ready to sign.
  • All the numbers are written down, not just discussed Rate, total, deposit method, invoice timing. Words, not screenshots.
  • I know exactly when I get paid — and it has a date “On approval” is a fog. “Within 30 days of invoice” is a date.
  • Scope is specific enough to defend Rewards, revisions, and out-of-scope work all named. Vague scope is free work.
  • Who owns the work when it’s done? I understand it Copyright, license, credit. Common snag for writers and creators.
  • Nothing here lets payment be cancelled after I’ve delivered Kill-fee and refund clauses — read them twice, slowly.
  • If either side can walk away, I know what happens then Termination terms: notice period, final payment, returned files.
  • No promises I can’t keep (non-compete, exclusivity, deadlines) Exclusivity is easy to nod at and hard to live with.
  • Disputes: where and how would they actually be resolved? Governing law and venue. Boring until the day it isn’t.
  • I re-read the whole thing once, and nothing surprised me Fresh-eyes pass. Surprises are questions, not dealbreakers.
