Weekly Money Review

Twenty minutes on Sunday beats twenty hours at tax time.

The check-in

  • Got every receipt for this week into one place Pile, envelope, or photos on your phone — just make them findable.
  • Logged every expense from this week (use the Expense Log sheet) Missing one? Water under the bridge. Log it now anyway.
  • Sent this week’s invoices No invoice, no income. Slow invoicing is quiet self-underpayment.
  • Noted payments that came in and marked those invoices paid Match each payment to an invoice number so nothing drifts.
  • Checked who owes me and nudged anything overdue A short, friendly reminder is a normal thing real businesses send.
  • Glanced at the bank balance and wrote it down below Not a goal, just a snapshot. Snapshots kill surprises.
  • Put aside a slice of income for taxes (even 10–20% helps) Separate account or envelope — future-you says thanks.
  • Looked ahead: anything due to me (or by me) next week? Deadlines, renewals, subscriptions, contract dates.
  • One number I’m watching this week is written down below Pick exactly one. Attention, not anxiety.
  • Next review is on my calendar (same ritual, on purpose) Deciding “when” now is the whole trick.
