# Indiana-LLC-lite notes (links only — not legal advice)

A plain-English starting list of *official* links for an Indiana LLC, gathered
the way a friend would gather them. This page is **not legal advice** and this
repository is **not affiliated with the State of Indiana** — these are starting
links so you can read the actual sources yourself.

## Forming / keeping an Indiana LLC in good standing

- Indiana Secretary of State business services portal (search, file, pay):
  https://bsd.sos.in.gov/BusinessSearch
- Indiana SOS "Business Services Division" info pages:
  https://www.in.gov/sos/business/
- Indiana.gov "Steps to form an LLC" guide (state-authored overview):
  https://www.in.gov/sos/business/corporations/limited-liability-companies/
- Registered agent requirements (Indiana Code, Title 23, Business and Other
  Associations; LLC article is Title 23, Article 18):
  https://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2023/ic/titles/23/

## Federal-side basics (same links an LLC anywhere needs)

- IRS: Get an Employer Identification Number (EIN) — free, direct from the IRS:
  https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number
- IRS: Self-employed individuals / contractors tax hub:
  https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed
- SBA: small business learning center (free, no-signup courses):
  https://www.sba.gov/learning-center

## Timely filing reminders that matter in Indiana

- Indiana SOS Business Services — biennial report and business entity info:
  https://www.in.gov/sos/business/4505.htm
- Indiana Department of Revenue — business taxpayer center:
  https://www.in.gov/dor/business-tax/

## How we keep this page useful instead of wrong

- Links point at **official state/federal pages only** — no paywalled
  "formation service" upsells hiding behind how-to articles.
- Pages above change; if a link reddens, the *topic* words in the heading are
  exactly what to search on the state's homepage.
- For anything with consequence (operating agreements, taxes, S-corps, hiring):
  a CPA or an Indiana-licensed attorney beats any listicle, including this one.
